Beyond ReLeaf Topical Cream

Beyond ReLeaf - is a topical cream product line that is designed to soothe muscles and joints. All products  provide a rapid absorbing and long-lasting formulation for the face or body. This line of creams is powered using high concentrations of CBD, that targets not only transient receptor potential (TRP) governing pain, but also the endocannabinoid system (ECS) receptors that affect peripheral inflammation. This product line was created to serve those with active lifestyles as well as those who are trying to improve their mobility. All our products are Paraben-free, non-staining fresh-scented vanishing creams. They are manufactured using cGMP standards in the USA and start with high-purity, natural bio-actives. Beyond ReLeaf has patents pending on its proprietary penetration and sustained release technologies.
