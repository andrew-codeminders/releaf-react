About Us

Committed to Quality

Quality should never be a compromise! Our goal is to provide products you can trust. Beyond ReLeaf designs every product to be highly effective, easy to use, long-lasting, and rapidly absorbed. We assure these attributes through strict testing of all raw materials as well as the final product, ensuring that every jar will provide the same pleasant experience that delivers on its claims. All our products are phthalate-free, paraben-free, and cruelty-free.

Science-based, Highly Effective AestheticalProducts

Beyond ReLeaf produces consistent, safe, high-quality products utilizing highly purified bio-active compounds. These compounds are selected and formulated to either act alone or in concert with other bio-actives to hit specific tissue receptors and deliver enhanced results. All bio-actives are vetted through peer-reviewed science, where their regulatory status, stability, safety, concentration and absorption kinetics are taken into account during the design of the  products. They utilize patent-pending topical delivery systems as well as unique combinations of bio-actives. 

Using Highest Manufacturing Standard!

We care about our customers, and we want you to be completely satisfied with your experience.   We focus on safety-first. including enhanced QC/QA, to produce consistent products that are pharmaceutical-grade . Many of our bio-actives are qualified using ISO17025 compliant laboratory testing. All production adheres to  strict cGMP manufacturing standards. Each product is backed by a C of A, shelf-life testing and follows Federal Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act guidelines.  All our products are proudly made in the USA

Research & Development

Cannabidiol (CBD) – Anti-inflammatory,   topical pain reliever  


Menthol – Reduces sensations by binding to the TRPM8 receptors, also responsible for cold sensation, thus reducing pain.  


Camphor – Acts as a slight analgesic by stimulating heat receptors TRPV3 and TRPV1 as well as cool receptor TRPM8  


Arnica Montana Flower Extract – Contains Helenalin, shown to reduce inflammation by inhibiting LTC4 Synthase, leading to a reduction in Leukotriene production.      


Avena Sativa (Oat) Kernel Extract –  Anti-inflammatory, moisturizing, and barrier-protective functions  


Niacinamide –  Also known as vitamin B3 and nicotinamide, niacinamide is a water-soluble vitamin that works with the natural substances in your skin to help visibly improve enlarged pores, uneven skin tone, fine lines and wrinkles, dullness, and a weakened surface and enhance skin’s barrier. 


* - not all listed ingredients are in every product

WHY BEYOND RELEAF PRODUCTS WORK

Penetration – Science-based formulation design focused on bringing the hemp extract-based bio-actives to their site of action to reduce the discomfort of muscles, joints, and skin.


Concentrated – designed to have a high concentration of hemp extract necessary to relieve joint and muscle discomfort from all activity levels, from intense physical workouts to normal discomfort due to aging.   


Purity – highly purified, single-focus hemp extract provides superior effectiveness without unintended side effects.   

Real Entourage Effect – Added ingredients increase the effectiveness of the hemp extract. These effects include the increased absorption, duration of action (up to 6 hours), and stability and shelf-life of hemp extract.


Luxury of Design – formulated by master formulators to provide a luxury experience of touch and smell. Rapid-penetration formulation rubs in quickly and then vanishes.   


Manufacturing Quality – Manufactured by following cGMP guidelines to ensure the same high-quality product every time. Each batch is tested to ensure it passes our rigorous requirements set forth by our certification of analysis. Each formulation is supported by shelf-life testing for the freshest product.
